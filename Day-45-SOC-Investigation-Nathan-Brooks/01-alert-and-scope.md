# 01 - Alert Scope and Investigation Questions

## Scope
The investigation focused on activity involving Nathan Brooks across Windows authentication, Active Directory group membership, workstation process telemetry, and SMB share access.

## Questions
1. What account and workstation were involved?
2. What authentication activity occurred?
3. Was there relevant group membership or authorization activity?
4. Which SMB resources were accessed?
5. What happened to `nathan brooks notes.txt`?
6. Can the file operation be tied to a specific process?
7. Is malicious intent supported by the available evidence?

## Analyst Approach
The investigation followed an evidence-first workflow:

`Evidence -> Observation -> Hypothesis -> Correlation -> Confidence -> Conclusion`
